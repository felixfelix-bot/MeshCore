=== Hand-worked example: 22 C / 50 % RH ===
  ln(RH/100) = ln(0.50) = -0.693147
  A*T/(B+T) = 17.625*22/(243.04+22) = 1.462987
  numerator   = 243.04*(-0.693147+1.462987) = 187.101801
  denominator = 17.625--0.693147-1.462987 = 16.855160
  Td = 187.101801/16.855160 = 11.10 C
