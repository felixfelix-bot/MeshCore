3.5 mm / 2.92 mm connectors: the NAME is the air-line inner
diameter of the mated interface, NOT a thread size. Thread group
is 1/4-36 UNS (same as SMA; compatible with SMA, 3.5 mm is an
air dielectric precision descendant; 2.92 mm (K) is the 40 GHz
descendant with the same thread group).

Air-line center-conductor diameter (50 ohm, air dielectric):
  b/a = e^(50/60) = 2.3010
  3.5 mm connector: b = 3.5 mm -> center pin a = 3.5/2.3010 = 1.5211 mm
  2.92 mm (K): b = 2.92 mm -> center pin a = 2.92/2.3010 = 1.2690 mm
  (These are the namesake dimensions: 3.5 mm and 2.92 mm are the
  OUTER conductor IDs of their mated air lines; the center pin
  follows from the 50-ohm impedance. REFERENCE arithmetic only.)

==========================================================================
B. GEOMETRIC AUDITS — sanity of hex-vs-thread relationships
==========================================================================

B1. SMA: hex AF 7.94 mm vs thread major 6.35 mm -> radial clearance
    per side = (7.94 - 6.35)/2 = 0.795 mm (hex AF > thread major: geometrically consistent, hex encloses thread)

B2. TNC hex audit (NEW FINDING): committed identification kit
    (chamber-rf-feedthroughs:143) lists TNC hex nut AF '~3/8 in
    ~ 9.5 mm'. A regular hexagon with AF 9.5 mm has AC = 10.97 mm,
    but it must SURROUND a thread whose major diameter is 11.11 mm:
    9.5 < 11.11 is geometrically IMPOSSIBLE (the flats of the nut
    would lie INSIDE the thread major circle). General rule: a hex
    AF must EXCEED the thread major diameter it encloses.

    Quantitative correction from the kit's own consistent data: a
    hex nut wall = (AF - thread major)/2. Committed SMA row:
    (7.94 - 6.35)/2 = 0.795 mm per side;
    committed N row: (17.4625 - 15.875)/2 = 0.794 mm per side;
    both imply wall ~= 0.8 mm per side. Applying the same wall to
    TNC: 11.1125 + 2 x 0.79 = 12.69 mm AF =
    1/2 in class — the class-typical TNC coupling/jam-nut hex.
    The committed 9.5 mm value is therefore an ERROR OF ARITHMETIC
    OR TRANSCRIPTION in the identification-kit table; this sheet
    carries 12.7 mm AF (1/2 in) as the class-typical TNC value,
    flags the source line, and keeps the corrected value labeled
    INDUSTRY-TYPICAL, UNVERIFIED like the rest of the kit.

B3. Discrimination margins (thread major OD):
    SMA 6.35 mm vs TNC 11.11 mm: gap = 4.76 mm (2.38 mm radial) — unambiguous at caliper
    precision.
    TNC 11.11 mm vs N 15.875 mm: gap = 4.76 mm —
    unambiguous.
    SMA 6.35 mm vs N 15.875 mm: gap = 9.53 mm —
    unambiguous. A miscount of TPI (36 vs 28 vs 24) can never
    rescue a wrong thread-OD match: the two discriminators agree
    or the reading is wrong.

B4. RP (reverse-polarity) families share the SAME thread as their
    parent: RP-SMA = 1/4-36 UNS (6.35 mm), RP-N = 5/8-24
    (15.875 mm), RP-TNC = 7/16-28 (11.11 mm), RP-BNC = the same
    2-lug bayonet.
    Therefore NO caliper measurement of thread or hex can separate
    standard from RP — only the center-contact configuration
    (look INTO the mated pair: pin+socket pair vs the RP
    socket+socket and pin+pin pairs) distinguishes them. This is
    the top procurement hazard H2 of bench/connector-adapter-bom.

B5. SMA vs 3.5 mm/2.92 mm (K): same 1/4-36 UNS thread group;
    dielectric material differs (PTFE vs mostly air) but the
    external geometry overlap means they mate; distinguishing by
    eye is unreliable — rely on markings/known provenance.

==========================================================================
C. U.FL / MHF FAMILY — diameter variation among MHF1/2/3/4
==========================================================================

The name 'U.FL' is Hirose's trademark; I-PEX MHF is the cross-
licensed equivalent; 'U.FL-style' clones are ubiquitous. The
mating interface of MHF1 == U.FL class is ONE standard, but the
plug-side RECEPTACLE body diameter (the stamped metal shell of
the board receptacle) varies among the GENERATIONS MHF1/2/3/4:

  MHF1 (U.FL class, ~1990s): receptacle body OD ~ 2.6-2.8 mm;
    mating height ~1.2-1.4 mm; the classic 'tiny 3 mm press-fit'
    footprint impression (committed: connector-adapter-bom H4
    'U.FL is a tiny 3 mm press-fit' — approximate, REFERENCE).
  MHF2: smaller (~ 1.6-2.0 mm receptacle OD class), lower profile
    (~1.0 mm mating height class); NOT mating-compatible with MHF1.
  MHF3: smallest generation (~1.6 mm receptacle OD class, mating
    height ~0.9 mm); NOT compatible with MHF1 or MHF2.
  MHF4: intermediate (~1.6-2.5 mm class); industry-typical
    dimensions, UNVERIFIED — no Hirose/I-PEX datasheet on host.

ALL MHF1/2/3/4 numbers in this section are INDUSTRY-TYPICAL,
UNVERIFIED: no Hirose or I-PEX datasheet exists on this host and
no outbound fetch is permitted (standing constraint; nothing
attempted). They are included because the task asks for the
genuine-diameter variation to be NOTED, not as procurement data.
The committed project anchor that IS on record: the ESP32-C3
SuperMini carrier carries 'U.FL (I-PEX) for external antenna'
(esp32c3-mini-v1.json:29) — i.e. MHF1-CLASS, the full-size U.FL
interface; physical population on c3-a UNVERIFIED (dut-board-
identity U5). Any pigtail bought for it must be U.FL/MHF1-class;
an MHF2/3/4 plug will not mate.
