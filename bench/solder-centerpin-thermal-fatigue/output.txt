
== 1. CTE mismatch ratio: pin alloy vs PTFE (order-of-magnitude deliverable) ==
  PTFE/pin(brass): 5.3x..7.1x (midpoint 6.2x); dCTE 81..116 ppm/K
  PTFE/pin(stainless): 5.9x..7.9x (midpoint 6.9x); dCTE 83..118 ppm/K
  PTFE/pin(copper): 5.9x..7.9x (midpoint 6.9x); dCTE 83..118 ppm/K
  brass-vs-stainless pin spread: 2 ppm/K (material choice of pin alloy is a second-order term)

== 2. Differential free strain per swing, pin-vs-PTFE ==
  -20 C cycle (dT=45 K): free strain 0.44% (4432 ue); axial slip over 5 mm bead 22.2 um; diametral gap change at 1 mm bore radius 4.4 um
  -40 C cycle (dT=65 K): free strain 0.64% (6402 ue); axial slip over 5 mm bead 32.0 um; diametral gap change at 1 mm bore radius 6.4 um

== 3. What the mismatch implies for shear/stress: compliant-member bound ==
  If the differential strain were FULLY CLAMPED into each member (sigma=E*da*dT):
    fully-clamped sigma(brass pin) over 65 K = 640 MPa
    fully-clamped sigma(stainless pin) over 65 K = 1236 MPa
    fully-clamped sigma(PTFE) over 65 K = 3 MPa
  brass yield is ~350-500 MPa class [INDUSTRY-TYPICAL]: a clamped pin would
  yield -> the stack CANNOT stay clamped; it must relieve. The reliever is the
  softest member: PTFE, modulus 200x lower than brass.
  Delivered stress bound at the pin/PTFE interface: ~3.2 MPa
  (PTFE shear/flow strength ~7-25 MPa class [INDUSTRY-TYPICAL]: the interface
   slides/creeps at low stress instead of storing load -> the ~6x CTE mismatch
   appears as um-scale micro-slip, NOT as stress delivered into the pin or its
   solder joint. Modulus ratio brass/PTFE = 200x, stainless/PTFE = 386x.
